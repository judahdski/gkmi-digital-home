Summary of What Was Built
Product Positioning & Design System:
Visual Language: Warm alabaster paper canvas (#FAF8F5), deep charcoal typography (#1C1917), and muted terracotta accents (#A34828), with an editorial typography pairing of Lora (serif) and Plus Jakarta Sans.
Documentary Imagery: Integrated photography depicting worship, fellowship, pastoral leadership, and devotional study.
Zero-Pill Metadata: Information, categories, scriptures, and reading times are presented as clean unboxed text with subtle typographic separators (·).
Homepage & Visitor Journey:
Hero Section: Highlights «Come as you are. Grow in faith. Walk together.» and «A Christian community where we gather to worship, grow in faith, serve others, and walk through life together.»
Operational Utility Ribbon (This Week): Compact Sunday worship schedule, [SERVICE TIME], [CHURCH ADDRESS], and quick directions.
You're Welcome Here & First-Time Visitor FAQ: 3 welcome pillars (Come As You Are, Worship Together, Find Your Community) and an accessible accordion answering questions on attire, coming alone, service length, and worship flow.
Meet Our Pastor Section: Thoughtful portrait, pastoral philosophy quote («My calling is not simply to preach, but to walk with people through every season of life.»), and direct connection options.
Pastoral Care Gateways: Two cards for Need Prayer? and Talk With Our Pastor.
Spiritual Content Hub Preview & Exploring Faith: Curated devotionals alongside an accessible seeker section answering questions like "Who is Jesus?" and "Can I come to church if I'm not a Christian?".
Upcoming Events & Final Invitation: Clear calendar cards and an inclusive closing invitation.
Spiritual Content Hub ("Grow in Faith"):
Filterable categories (Devotional, Bible Reflection, Prayer, Faith & Life, and Exploring Faith) with real-time search by keyword, scripture, or topic.
Article Detail View: Complete editorial reading layout constrained to 65–75ch, drop caps, scripture quotation block, reflection body, practical daily application, personal prayer box, and share actions.
Pastoral Care & Confidential Forms:
Prayer Request Flow: Respectful form with optional name/contact fields, required prayer text, follow-up preferences (WhatsApp/Phone/Email), confidentiality notice, and a warm confirmation state.
Pastoral Counseling Flow: Topic selectors (Spiritual, Family, Marriage, Career, Struggles, Grief), meeting method options (In Person, WhatsApp, Phone, Online), and strict pastoral confidentiality safeguards.
Plan Your Visit Modal: Lightweight helper for first-timers to notify the welcome team.
Bilingual Support & Responsiveness:
Instant toggle between Indonesian (ID) and English (EN) with local persistence.
Responsive navigation and a subtle mobile sticky action bar (Visit Us / Need Prayer?) respecting mobile viewport boundaries.
